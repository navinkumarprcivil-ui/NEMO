Delete Your Account & Data

Applies to the Nemo Aqua Store app & www.nemoaquastore.in · Last updated: 17 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Nemo Aqua Store account and the data associated with it. Nemo Aqua Store is operated by Nemo Aqua Store, Salem & Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

How to request deletion

Send us a deletion request using either method below, from the phone number or email linked to your account:

  1. 💬 WhatsApp us at +91 93609 21030, or
  2. ✉️ Email nemoaquastore@gmail.com

Use the subject/message "Delete my account" and include the name, phone number and/or email you used in the app so we can locate your account. We verify the request comes from you, then process it within 7 days and confirm once done.

💬 Request on WhatsApp ✉️ Request by Email

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your profile (name, phone, email, saved delivery addresses)
  • Your wishlist, cart and any wallet / loyalty points and referral balances
  • Reviews, ratings and tank-showcase photos you posted
  • Payment-proof screenshots you submitted

What we keep, and for how long

Completed order and payment records (invoice, items, amount, delivery details) are retained for as long as required by Indian tax and accounting law — typically up to 6 years — after which they are deleted or anonymised. This is a legal requirement we cannot waive while the retention period applies. We do not use retained records for any other purpose.

Deleting only some of your data

You don't have to delete your whole account. You can also ask us to delete specific data — for example a review or tank photo you posted, or a saved address — using the same WhatsApp or email contact above. Just tell us what you'd like removed.
